Risk & Futures

Risk & Futures maps weak signals, institutional blind spots, plausible scenarios, and the decision windows around emerging risks.

Why this area matters

The value of foresight is not prediction. It is clearer interpretation of signals before consequences become locked in.

Methodological note

Scenario work is labelled as inference, not forecast, and is tied back to evidence, boundaries, and decision relevance.
