Institutions & Governance

Institutions & Governance studies how rules, incentives, standards, and operating models shape public-interest decisions.

Why this area matters

Governance failures often occur between policy intent and practical use. This area focuses on the translation layer where institutions turn analysis into action.

Methodological note

Outputs distinguish governance structure, decision context, and implementation constraints rather than treating policy as a finished endpoint.

Framework

Decision Usability Framework

Define a practical structure for assessing whether a policy, guidance document, or governance output can support decisions at the point of use.

Framework

Institutional Translation Taxonomy

Create a public vocabulary for describing how institutional knowledge moves from signal to interpretation, decision, and consequence.

Methodology Note

Assessing Decision Usability in AI Governance Documents

Define a method for reviewing governance and policy materials without claiming validated measurement.

Framework

Decision Brief Architecture

Design a reusable structure for public decision briefs that separate context, signal, interpretation, limits, and decision relevance.
