AI & Technology

AI & Technology examines technical capability, infrastructure, model behaviour, and the institutional conditions that shape how AI systems become usable or risky.

Why this area matters

Technical change only becomes consequential when it meets incentives, workflows, policy, and human interpretation. This area keeps capability signals connected to decisions and consequences.

Methodological note

Analysis separates observed technical signals from institutional inference and avoids unsupported claims about capability, adoption, or impact.
