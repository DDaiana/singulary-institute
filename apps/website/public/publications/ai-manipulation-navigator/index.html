AI Manipulation Navigator

Create a prevention-focused public framework for navigating AI-enabled influence risks without presenting clinical, legal, or direct-support advice.

Framework · Risk & Futures

Central idea

This framework will provide a prevention-focused public structure for navigating AI-enabled influence risks.

What this output explores

It will cover risk categories, public interpretation boundaries, responsible language, and areas requiring further review.

Current scope

This output is an early public version intended to clarify the core idea. It does not present original empirical findings and may be expanded as the research develops.
