Decision Usability Framework

Define a practical structure for assessing whether a policy, guidance document, or governance output can support decisions at the point of use.

Framework · Institutions & Governance

Central idea

This framework will define a practical structure for assessing whether policy and governance materials can support decisions at the point of use.

What this output explores

It will cover decision context, usability criteria, interpretation boundaries, and examples of how public guidance can become more actionable.

Current scope

This output is an early public version intended to clarify the core idea. It does not present original empirical findings and may be expanded as the research develops.
