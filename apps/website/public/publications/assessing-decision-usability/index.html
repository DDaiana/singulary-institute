Assessing Decision Usability in AI Governance Documents

Define a method for reviewing governance and policy materials without claiming validated measurement.

Methodology Note · Institutions & Governance

Central idea

This methodology note will outline how Singulary reviews governance and policy materials for decision usability without presenting a validated measurement instrument.

What this output explores

It will cover review criteria, public scope boundaries, evidence boundaries, and future development needs.

Current scope

This output is an early public version intended to clarify the core idea. It does not present original empirical findings and may be expanded as the research develops.
