# Publication Trust Levels

## Developing

Requirements:

- project or draft output exists
- evidence may be incomplete or not yet reviewed

Evidence expectations:

- no findings claimed without sources
- source registry may be empty or in preparation

Review expectations:

- internal review pending

## Supported

Requirements:

- source material documented
- evidence registry exists
- claims linked to sources

Evidence expectations:

- source trail visible
- limitations identified

Review expectations:

- internal evidence review completed

## Verified

Requirements:

- evidence review completed
- contradictions and limitations addressed
- editorial review completed

Evidence expectations:

- traceable evidence chain
- source quality assessed

Review expectations:

- editorial approval recorded

## Peer-Reviewed

Requirements:

- external review documented

Evidence expectations:

- source trail and review trail complete

Review expectations:

- external reviewer, review date, and outcome recorded

## Archived

Requirements:

- publication or closure rationale recorded
- archive status complete

Evidence expectations:

- evidence status preserved

Review expectations:

- archive review completed

## Publication Safeguard

No publication may be classified as Evidence-Based unless sources are documented, an evidence registry exists, and evidence review has been completed.

No publication may be classified as Peer-Reviewed unless external review is documented.

## Research Mode Integration

Interpretive publications may achieve:

- Developing
- Supported
- Verified

Primary research publications may achieve:

- Developing
- Supported
- Verified
- Peer-Reviewed

Peer-Reviewed status requires external review documentation regardless of research mode.
