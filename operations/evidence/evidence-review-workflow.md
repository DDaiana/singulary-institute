# Evidence Review Workflow

## Safeguard

ChatGPT assists in identifying, organizing, reviewing, extracting, and synthesizing evidence from traceable sources provided through research workflows.

ChatGPT does not generate evidence, source material, observations, findings, or datasets.

## Workflow

Research Question

-> Evidence Identification

-> Evidence Collection from traceable source material

-> Evidence Registry

-> Evidence Review

-> Evidence Synthesis

-> Interpretive Framework

-> Publication Draft

## Required Controls

- no publication may claim findings without sources
- no evidence-based publication without evidence registry
- no project may reach Published without evidence review
- no publication may receive trust classification without evidence classification
