# Research Integrity & Evidence Policy

Singulary Institute uses AI-assisted research workflows.

AI systems may assist with:

- interpretation
- synthesis
- organization
- classification
- framework development

AI systems do not constitute evidence.

Research claims, findings, and evidence classifications must be supported by documented source material and traceable review processes.

ChatGPT may assist in identifying, organizing, reviewing, extracting, and synthesizing evidence from traceable sources provided through research workflows.

ChatGPT may not generate evidence, fabricate observations, fabricate source material, fabricate findings, fabricate datasets, or create unsupported factual claims.
