# Evidence Classification

## Conceptual

Definition:

Framework, model, taxonomy, or hypothesis.

Requirements:

- clearly labelled as conceptual
- no claim of findings
- no claim of evidence-based status

## Interpretive

Definition:

Source-informed synthesis or analytical interpretation.

Requirements:

- source material identified or planned
- interpretation distinguished from evidence
- limitations documented

## Evidence-Based

Definition:

Publication or output with a traceable evidence chain and explicit sources.

Requirements:

- sources documented
- evidence registry exists
- evidence review completed
- evidence synthesis completed

## Peer-Reviewed

Definition:

Externally reviewed work.

Requirements:

- evidence-based or equivalent source trail
- external review documented
- review outcome recorded
