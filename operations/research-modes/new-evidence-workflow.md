# Generation of New Evidence Workflow

Research Question

-> Research Director

-> Programme Architect

-> Evidence Generation Approval

-> Research Design

-> Data Collection Plan

-> Evidence Collection

Examples:

- interviews
- surveys
- fieldwork
- experiments
- direct observation
- original datasets
- participant engagement

-> Evidence Registry

-> Evidence Review

-> Evidence Synthesis

-> Editorial Review

-> Publication Draft

-> Codex Execution OS

-> Publication

## Safeguard

No project may be classified as Generation of New Evidence without approved research design and evidence generation requirements.
