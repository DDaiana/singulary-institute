# Research Mode Classification

## Mode A: Interpretation of Existing Evidence

Definition:

Research primarily based on:

- reports
- papers
- books
- datasets
- policy documents
- institutional publications
- literature reviews

Outputs:

- frameworks
- syntheses
- typologies
- models
- strategic assessments
- interpretive publications

Evidence pathway:

Existing sources are identified, organized, reviewed, extracted, compared, and synthesized. The project does not generate new primary evidence.

Trust pathway:

Developing -> Supported -> Verified.

## Mode B: Generation of New Evidence

Definition:

Research involving:

- surveys
- interviews
- fieldwork
- experiments
- original datasets
- direct observation
- participant engagement

Outputs:

- original findings
- primary research
- original evidence

Evidence pathway:

Primary evidence may be generated only after research design, evidence collection method, data source plan, review process, and expected outputs are documented and approved.

Trust pathway:

Developing -> Supported -> Verified -> Peer-Reviewed, if external review is documented.
