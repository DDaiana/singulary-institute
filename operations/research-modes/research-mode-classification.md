# Research Mode Classification

## Mode A: Interpretation of Existing Evidence

Definition:

Research primarily based on:

- reports
- papers
- books
- datasets
- policy documents
- institutional publications
- literature reviews

Outputs:

- frameworks
- syntheses
- typologies
- models
- strategic assessments
- interpretive publications

Evidence pathway:

Existing sources are identified, organized, reviewed, extracted, compared, and synthesized. The project does not generate new primary evidence.

Trust pathway:

Developing -> Supported -> Verified.

## Mode B: Hybrid Research Mode

Definition:

A project that is primarily interpretive but becomes funded, partnered, or operationally supported in a way that allows limited new evidence activities without becoming a full primary research project.

Use when:

- the project is based mainly on existing reports, policy documents, literature, and public sources
- funding or partnership exists
- limited interviews, surveys, contributor observations, or practitioner feedback may be added
- outputs remain mainly interpretive intelligence with supplementary original inputs

Outputs:

- interpretive intelligence
- source-informed synthesis
- frameworks
- models
- limited supplementary input notes, if approved

Hybrid outputs must distinguish:

- existing evidence synthesis
- supplementary original input
- interpretation
- limitations

Hybrid Mode is not the same as full Generation of New Evidence.

Trust pathway:

Developing -> Supported -> Verified. Peer-Reviewed may apply only if external review is documented and evidence requirements are met.

## Funded Interpretive Intelligence Project

Definition:

A project with approved funding, grant support, or institutional backing that remains based on interpretation of existing evidence.

Funding may support:

- research time
- source review
- publication production
- dissemination
- design
- contributor coordination
- platform maintenance

Funding does not generate new evidence unless original data collection is explicitly included.

Classification:

- Research Mode: Interpretation of Existing Evidence
- Funding Status: approved, if public-safe and true
- Evidence Generation Required: no

## Mode C: Generation of New Evidence

Definition:

Research involving:

- surveys
- interviews
- fieldwork
- experiments
- original datasets
- direct observation
- participant engagement

Outputs:

- original findings
- primary research
- original evidence

Evidence pathway:

Primary evidence may be generated only after research design, evidence collection method, data source plan, review process, and expected outputs are documented and approved.

Trust pathway:

Developing -> Supported -> Verified -> Peer-Reviewed, if external review is documented.
