# Publication Clearance Checklist

Before content is committed publicly, verify:

- no private strategy notes
- no personal financial information
- no sensitive grant detail
- no unsupported claims
- no confidential source material
- no private ChatGPT reasoning
- no unpublished internal assessment unless intentionally public
- research mode is correctly labelled
- funding visibility setting is respected
- publication clearance status is cleared or intentionally public-safe
