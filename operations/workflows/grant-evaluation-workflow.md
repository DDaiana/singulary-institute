# Grant Evaluation Workflow

## Input

Grant opportunity.

## Sequence

1. Research Director identifies strategic relevance.
2. Programme Architect maps programme and project fit.
3. Grant Strategist evaluates fit score, requirements, deliverables, and funding pathway.
4. Editorial Director maps required outputs.
5. Institutional Auditor checks for grant-chasing risk and repository distortion.

## Output

- fit score
- requirements
- funding pathway
- recommended action

## Exit Criteria

The grant may proceed only if it strengthens the existing research architecture.
