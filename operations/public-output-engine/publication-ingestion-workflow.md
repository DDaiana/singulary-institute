# Publication Ingestion Workflow

```text
Research Question
  ↓
ChatGPT Research OS
  ↓
Knowledge Asset
  ↓
Editorial Review
  ↓
Approved Publication
  ↓
Codex Implementation Package
  ↓
Codex Execution OS
  ↓
Repository
  ↓
Website
  ↓
Archive
```

The Research OS is the intellectual source.

The Execution OS integrates approved publication packages into the repository, registry, website, and archive.
