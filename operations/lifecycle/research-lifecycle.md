# Master Research Lifecycle

## Stage 0: Idea

An initial idea, question, opportunity, grant, partnership, or strategic initiative exists but has not yet been assessed.

## Stage 1: Strategic Assessment

The Research Director evaluates strategic value, institutional fit, risks, assumptions, and priority.

## Stage 2: Programme Placement

The Programme Architect determines research area alignment, programme placement, project placement, and whether new structure is required.

## Stage 3: Research Architecture

The research architecture is designed: questions, methodology, dependencies, expected outputs, and registry relationships are defined.

## Stage 4: Research in Motion

The project is active. Signals, sources, methodology, frameworks, and draft structures are being developed.

## Stage 5: Draft Output

A publication or output exists in draft form but has not completed editorial review.

## Stage 6: Editorial Review

The Editorial Director reviews claims, structure, evidence, public positioning, archive readiness, and publication status.

## Stage 7: Published

The output is approved for public release and appears in the website/publication system.

## Stage 8: Archived

The output, project, or initiative is archived with publication record or closure rationale.

## Movement Rule

No item may advance stages without updating status, owner, last updated date, and next required action.
