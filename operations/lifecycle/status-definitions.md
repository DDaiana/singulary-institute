# Status Definitions

## Idea

Purpose: Capture a possible initiative before institutional review.

Entry Criteria: A question, opportunity, grant, partnership, publication idea, or project idea is proposed.

Exit Criteria: Research Director assessment begins.

Required Next Action: Run Strategic Assessment.

## Under Review

Purpose: Hold work while role-based governance evaluates fit and risk.

Entry Criteria: The idea enters operations review.

Exit Criteria: Proceed, revise, pause, or reject decision is recorded.

Required Next Action: Complete relevant role assessments.

## Approved

Purpose: Confirm the initiative has institutional fit and may be architected or implemented.

Entry Criteria: Research Director and relevant roles approve the direction.

Exit Criteria: Programme placement and research architecture are completed.

Required Next Action: Define or update programme/project architecture.

## Research Architecture Complete

Purpose: Confirm the work has placement, questions, methodology requirements, expected outputs, and dependencies.

Entry Criteria: Programme Architect completes placement and architecture.

Exit Criteria: Research activity begins.

Required Next Action: Move into research in motion with owner and next action.

## Research in Motion

Purpose: Track active investigation without implying completed findings.

Entry Criteria: Research architecture is complete and work is underway.

Exit Criteria: A draft output is produced, the project is paused, superseded, cancelled, or archived.

Required Next Action: Develop methodology, sources, framework, or draft output.

## Drafting

Purpose: Track outputs that exist in draft form but are not final.

Entry Criteria: A publication or output draft exists.

Exit Criteria: Editorial review begins.

Required Next Action: Prepare draft for editorial review.

## Editorial Review

Purpose: Review output quality, evidence, claims, public positioning, and archive readiness.

Entry Criteria: Draft output is ready for review.

Exit Criteria: Publish, revise, pause, cancel, or archive decision.

Required Next Action: Complete editorial review and record decision.

## Published

Purpose: Mark outputs approved for public release.

Entry Criteria: Editorial review is complete and publication is approved.

Exit Criteria: Archive placement or revision cycle begins.

Required Next Action: Confirm website, archive, and registry records.

## Archived

Purpose: Preserve a completed, closed, or inactive item with rationale.

Entry Criteria: Publication exists or closure rationale is recorded.

Exit Criteria: None unless superseded by a new initiative.

Required Next Action: Maintain archive record.

## Paused

Purpose: Hold work intentionally without deleting it.

Entry Criteria: Work cannot proceed due to dependency, capacity, fit, or timing.

Exit Criteria: Resume, supersede, cancel, or archive decision.

Required Next Action: Record pause reason and review date.

## Superseded

Purpose: Mark work replaced by a newer project, programme, or output.

Entry Criteria: A successor structure or output is approved.

Exit Criteria: Archive or migrate references.

Required Next Action: Link successor and update affected registries.

## Cancelled

Purpose: Close work that should not proceed.

Entry Criteria: Governance review determines the work lacks fit, evidence, capacity, or priority.

Exit Criteria: Archive closure rationale if necessary.

Required Next Action: Record cancellation rationale and remove active next actions.
