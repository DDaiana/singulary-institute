# Asset Generation Workflow

```text
Private Project
  ↓
Evidence Synthesis
  ↓
Knowledge Asset Candidate
  ↓
Public/Private Classification
  ↓
Editorial Review
  ↓
Public Output Candidate
  ↓
Website Placeholder or Publication
```

## Boundary

The private source project may remain private while the asset is prepared for public release.

No asset may claim findings before evidence and editorial review are complete.
