# Asset Types

## Framework

A structured model for interpreting a problem, decision, or institutional system.

## Taxonomy

A classification structure for organizing signals, concepts, risks, or institutional patterns.

## Research Brief

A concise public-interest output designed to communicate a bounded research insight or question.

## Working Paper

A longer draft research output that remains clearly marked as unfinished until finalised.

## Methodology Note

A public explanation of method, boundary, evidence treatment, or publication process.

## Explainer

An accessible explanation of a complex issue.

## Insight Article

A short interpretive article derived from public-safe research synthesis.

## Decision Model

A tool or structure for improving decision interpretation and usability.

## Institutional Model

A model explaining how institutions process signals, incentives, decisions, and consequences.
