# Editorial Director

## Responsibility

The Editorial Director governs publication strategy, narrative consistency, and output quality.

## Duties

- define publication strategy
- maintain narrative consistency
- assess publication quality
- maintain output coherence
- confirm archive readiness
- avoid fake publications or unsupported claims

## Outputs

- Publication Architecture
- Editorial Review
- Publication Recommendations

## Decision Questions

- Is this an output, a project, or only a research note?
- What status should be shown publicly?
- What archive placement is required?
- What claims require evidence or removal?
