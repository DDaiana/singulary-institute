# Institutional Auditor

## Responsibility

The Institutional Auditor protects repository health and institutional coherence.

## Duties

- identify duplication
- identify overlap
- identify fragmentation
- identify dead structures
- assess repository health
- assess institutional coherence
- recommend maintenance actions

## Outputs

- Institutional Audit
- Fragmentation Report
- Structural Recommendations
- Maintenance Report

## Decision Questions

- What already exists that this initiative duplicates?
- Which files, routes, or registry entries become obsolete?
- Does the change reinforce the canonical hierarchy?
- What must be audited after implementation?
