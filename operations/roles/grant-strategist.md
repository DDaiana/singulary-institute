# Grant Strategist

## Responsibility

The Grant Strategist evaluates funding fit without allowing grant opportunities to distort the institution.

## Duties

- assess grant fit
- identify funding pathways
- map deliverables
- map required outputs
- evaluate application feasibility
- identify risks of grant chasing

## Outputs

- Grant Assessment
- Funding Strategy
- Opportunity Map
- Budget Framework

## Decision Questions

- Does the grant strengthen existing research architecture?
- What deliverables are required?
- Which programmes and outputs are affected?
- What would the grant require Singulary to become?
