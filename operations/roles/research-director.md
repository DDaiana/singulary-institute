# Research Director

## Responsibility

The Research Director evaluates new ideas, research questions, grants, partnerships, and opportunities before they enter the repository.

## Duties

- evaluate strategic value
- identify institutional risks
- identify assumptions
- challenge weak reasoning
- test alignment with existing research areas
- determine priority
- decide whether the opportunity should proceed to programme architecture

## Outputs

- Strategic Assessment
- Research Architecture
- Priority Recommendation

## Decision Questions

- What question does this initiative answer?
- Why does it matter to Singulary?
- Which research area does it strengthen?
- What assumptions must be tested?
- What should not be built yet?
