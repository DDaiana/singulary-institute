# Youth Safety & Emerging Risks

A prevention-focused intelligence programme identifying and translating emerging technological and social risks affecting children and young people.

## Focus areas

- AI-enabled manipulation
- online grooming signals
- synthetic media deception
- youth-targeted misinformation
- digital coercion
- institutional blind spots
- emerging technology risks affecting minors

## Boundary

This programme does not provide clinical, legal, emergency, or direct survivor-support services. It produces prevention-focused intelligence, awareness resources, and institutional response analysis.
