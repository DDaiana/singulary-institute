# AI Use Policy

AI tools may assist with summarization, synthesis, classification, formatting, and drafting.

Human review is required before publication.

Singulary does not permit:

- fabricated citations;
- invented statistics;
- unsupported institutional claims;
- false partnerships;
- unverified source references.

All AI-assisted outputs must distinguish between:

1. source-supported fact;
2. analytical interpretation;
3. hypothesis or open question.
