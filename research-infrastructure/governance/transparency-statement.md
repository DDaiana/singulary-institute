# Transparency Statement

Singulary Institute separates evidence, interpretation, and recommendation. Public research should make clear what is known, what is inferred, what remains uncertain, and which institutional judgement has been applied.

## Commitments

- Identify research area, programme, status, and source path for each publication.
- Preserve draft, review, approved, website, LinkedIn, and archive state in the content registry.
- Distinguish signal detection from policy recommendation.
- Maintain traceability between research infrastructure, content, and the public website.

## Repository Practice

The repository is the authoritative institutional record. Website pages derive from structured content rather than hardcoded publication lists.
