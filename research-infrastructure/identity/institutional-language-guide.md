# Institutional Language Guide

## Use

- structured intelligence
- institutional analysis
- policy usability
- decision relevance
- operator relevance
- governance systems
- information compression
- institutional friction
- signal interpretation
- consequence mapping

## Avoid

- revolutionary
- viral
- hustle
- guru
- disruption
- growth hacking
- guaranteed outcomes
- panic framing

## Voice

Calm, precise, restrained, source-grounded.
