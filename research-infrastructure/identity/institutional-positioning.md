# Institutional Positioning

Singulary is an independent research and intelligence initiative focused on AI policy, innovation systems, and decision-ready synthesis under conditions of technological uncertainty.

It investigates how institutions, operators, and societies process high-volume technological change under stress.

Singulary does not position itself as a news outlet, startup, or commentary brand. It functions as a structured intelligence layer: detecting signals, interpreting mechanisms, and translating complexity into usable frameworks.
