# Publication Development Input

Asset: AI Manipulation Navigator

## Purpose

Create a prevention-focused public framework for navigating AI-enabled influence risks without presenting clinical, legal, or direct-support advice.

## Research Questions

- What public categories help institutions interpret AI-enabled influence risks?
- How can risks be described without overclaiming harm or evidence?
- Which signals might require further review before publication?

## Target Audience

Public-interest researchers, educators, safety teams, and youth-facing institutions.

## Expected Outputs

Framework package, website summary, and future research brief structure.

## Evidence Requirements

Public AI risk literature, digital safety materials, platform policy materials, and source review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Signal map, glossary, educator-facing explainer, and brief series.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
