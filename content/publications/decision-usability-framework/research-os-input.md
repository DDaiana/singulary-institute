# Publication Development Input

Asset: Decision Usability Framework

## Purpose

Define a practical structure for assessing whether a policy, guidance document, or governance output can support decisions at the point of use.

## Research Questions

- What makes an institutional output decision-usable?
- Which elements help a reader move from interpretation to action?
- Where should uncertainty, limits, and decision context be made visible?

## Target Audience

Policy teams, governance practitioners, researchers, and institutional decision-makers.

## Expected Outputs

Version 1 framework package, website summary, and future publication draft.

## Evidence Requirements

Public governance materials, policy documents, decision-support literature, and documented source-based synthesis after source review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Examples, scoring rubrics, annotated policy-output reviews, and comparative usability cases.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
