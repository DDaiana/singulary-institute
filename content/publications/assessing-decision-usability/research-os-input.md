# Publication Development Input

Asset: Assessing Decision Usability in AI Governance Documents

## Purpose

Define a method for reviewing governance and policy materials without claiming validated measurement.

## Research Questions

- How can decision usability be assessed without overclaiming evidence?
- Which public criteria can be reviewed consistently?
- How should limitations and uncertainty be documented?

## Target Audience

Research teams, editors, and policy analysts preparing public-facing reviews.

## Expected Outputs

Methodology note package and future checklist.

## Evidence Requirements

Methodology literature, policy-analysis practice, and explicit source documentation once reviewed.

## Future Expansion Paths

Review template, example assessments, and editorial quality controls.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
