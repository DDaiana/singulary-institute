# Research OS Input

Asset: AI Risk Signals for Young People

## Purpose

Prepare a public research brief structure for communicating AI risk signals affecting young people with methodological caution.

## Research Questions

- Which signals are suitable for a public brief?
- What should remain under review?
- How can a brief communicate risk without unsupported claims?

## Target Audience

Public-interest researchers, educators, youth-facing institutions, and grant reviewers.

## Expected Outputs

Research brief package and future Version 1 publication draft.

## Evidence Requirements

Public AI safety materials, youth digital risk literature, platform policy sources, and Research OS synthesis after review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Brief series, evidence table, public glossary, and methodology companion.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
