# Research OS Input

Asset: Decision Brief Architecture

## Purpose

Design a reusable structure for public decision briefs that separate context, signal, interpretation, limits, and decision relevance.

## Research Questions

- What should a decision brief contain?
- How can briefs avoid overstating evidence?
- How can a brief make uncertainty and next actions visible?

## Target Audience

Researchers, editors, programme teams, and institutional communicators.

## Expected Outputs

Framework package, brief architecture template, and future examples.

## Evidence Requirements

Public communication methods, policy brief formats, and Research OS synthesis after source review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Reusable brief template, annotated examples, and publication quality rubric.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
