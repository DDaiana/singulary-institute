# Publication Development Input

Asset: How AI Shapes Online Decisions

## Purpose

Explain how AI-mediated systems may shape online decision environments without claiming direct causal findings.

## Research Questions

- How can AI-mediated systems alter the choice environment?
- What is the difference between a decision aid and a decision pressure?
- How can public outputs discuss influence while preserving evidence boundaries?

## Target Audience

General readers, educators, public-interest teams, and policy communicators.

## Expected Outputs

Explainer package and future public article draft.

## Evidence Requirements

Public literature on recommender systems, online behaviour, interface design, and source-based synthesis after review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Decision environment map, examples, and companion methodology note.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
