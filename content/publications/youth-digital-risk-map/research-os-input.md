# Research OS Input

Asset: Youth Digital Risk Map

## Purpose

Develop a public map for organizing youth-facing digital risk signals in a prevention-focused and evidence-bounded way.

## Research Questions

- Which risk dimensions can be named without overclaiming evidence?
- How should weak signals be separated from findings?
- What categories help institutions identify prevention windows?

## Target Audience

Youth-facing organisations, public-interest researchers, educators, and safety teams.

## Expected Outputs

Taxonomy package, public risk map outline, and future research brief.

## Evidence Requirements

Youth safety literature, public digital risk frameworks, platform policy materials, and Research OS review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Signal categories, visual map, and brief series.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
