# AI-Enabled Manipulation

This working project tracks emerging manipulation risks made more scalable, adaptive, or persuasive through AI systems. The initial focus is youth safety, prevention, and institutional readiness.

## Research Question

How should institutions detect, describe, and reduce AI-enabled manipulation risks affecting children and young people?
