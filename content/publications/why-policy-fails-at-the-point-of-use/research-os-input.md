# Research OS Input

Asset: Why Policy Fails at the Point of Use

## Purpose

Develop a Version 1 working paper about why formal policy outputs may fail to become usable in real decision environments.

## Research Questions

- Where does policy intent lose usability?
- How do time, context, incentives, and interpretation affect institutional use?
- What translation structures could improve decision readiness?

## Target Audience

Policy researchers, public-interest funders, governance teams, and institutional leaders.

## Expected Outputs

Working paper package, publication outline, and archive-ready metadata.

## Evidence Requirements

Public policy documents, governance literature, implementation literature, and Research OS synthesis after documented review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Case examples, evidence review, diagrams, and public brief versions.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
