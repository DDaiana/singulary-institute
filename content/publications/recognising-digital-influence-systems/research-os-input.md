# Research OS Input

Asset: Recognising Digital Influence Systems

## Purpose

Create a public explainer about digital influence systems as environments that shape interpretation and decision-making.

## Research Questions

- What makes a digital environment influential?
- How should public readers distinguish influence, persuasion, manipulation, and recommendation?
- What signals should be treated cautiously until evidence is reviewed?

## Target Audience

Educators, researchers, public-interest organisations, and digital literacy audiences.

## Expected Outputs

Explainer package and future public article draft.

## Evidence Requirements

Digital literacy materials, platform governance materials, public research literature, and Research OS synthesis after source review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Diagram, classroom-facing version, and public checklist.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
