# Research OS Input

Asset: Institutional Translation Taxonomy

## Purpose

Create a public vocabulary for describing how institutional knowledge moves from signal to interpretation, decision, and consequence.

## Research Questions

- What forms of translation are needed between analysis and institutional action?
- Which translation failures make good analysis unusable?
- How can public outputs name the difference between information, interpretation, and decision support?

## Target Audience

Researchers, policy communicators, programme designers, and public-interest institutions.

## Expected Outputs

Taxonomy package, publication outline, and website summary.

## Evidence Requirements

Public institutional reports, governance literature, and source-based Research OS synthesis after review.

## Future Expansion Paths

Glossary, diagrams, sector examples, and taxonomy-based publication series.

## Safeguard

Do not generate fictional evidence, fabricate findings, or imply a completed study. Draft content must remain interpretive until evidence review and editorial approval are complete.
