# Publication Development Input

Explain synthetic media in accessible language while clarifying boundaries, uncertainty, and public-interest relevance.

- What is synthetic media?
- What distinctions matter for public understanding?
- How can the topic be explained without sensational claims?

General readers, educators, youth-facing organisations, and public-interest communicators.

Explainer package and future public article draft.

## Future Expansion Paths
