# AI-Enabled Manipulation

Programme: Youth Safety & Emerging Risks

Research areas: AI & Technology; Society & Behavior; Risk & Futures

Status: research in motion

## Overview

AI-Enabled Manipulation is a prevention-focused intelligence project on emerging AI-enabled manipulation risks affecting young people.

## Why It Matters

Youth-facing digital environments are changing quickly. Prevention-focused intelligence helps institutions identify weak signals before risks become normalised.

## Methodology

Signal collection, source review, scenario mapping, boundary checks, and interpretation are used to separate evidence, inference, and responsible analysis.

## Framework

The project maps risk signals across actor capability, target vulnerability, platform affordance, institutional visibility, and intervention window.

## Outputs

- Prevention-focused intelligence brief
- Signal taxonomy
- Methodological boundary note

## Boundary

Singulary does not present this work as a crisis, therapeutic, legal, or direct survivor-support service.
