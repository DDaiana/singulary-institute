# The Missing Layer

Programme: Policy Usability & Decision Systems

Research areas: AI & Technology; Institutions & Governance

Status: draft framework

## Overview

The Missing Layer investigates the gap between policy production and practical institutional decision-making.

## Why It Matters

Policy can be rigorous and still unusable if it does not reach the decision context in a form that supports judgement, timing, and action.

## Methodology

Policy-output review, decision-system mapping, interpretation of institutional friction, and framework development.

## Framework

The project maps the path from signal to interpretation, decision, and consequence, with emphasis on the translation layer.

## Outputs

- Decision-translation framework
- Programme-linked draft output
